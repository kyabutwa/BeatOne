# ZALAGREN KENYA — BEATMARKET + BEATBNB POLICY CONTROL BASELINE
Date: 2026-10-03
Jurisdiction: Kenya launch baseline

This is an engineering/compliance control baseline, not legal advice or a regulatory licence.

## BeatMarket

BeatMarket supports professional identity, careers, opportunities, business presence, storefronts, publishing, relationships and location-aware discovery.

### Data protection
The platform should classify processing by purpose and role:
- participant identity
- professional profile
- business/provider data
- communications
- GPS/location
- media
- transaction data
- sensitive data where a participant chooses to provide it

ODPC registration and controller/processor obligations must be assessed for the actual Zalagren entity and each processing role. ODPC's current portal explicitly requires classification of personal and sensitive data, purposes, transfers and safeguards. citeturn0search0

Location sharing is always purpose-bound and should support coarse, approximate, exact and temporary live-sharing states. Live location is not a public default.

### Professional and commercial claims
BeatMarket distinguishes:
- participant identity
- professional claim
- business registration evidence
- tax evidence
- sector licence evidence
- Zalagren platform verification

Zalagren verification must never be displayed as a government licence or professional-regulator certification.

### Tax/invoicing
Business participants need a tax/invoicing state. KRA states that persons engaged in business are required to onboard eTIMS and issue electronic tax invoices, with online, Lite and system-to-system options. citeturn1search0turn1search6

BeatMarket must not display "eTIMS verified" unless actual evidence is recorded.

### Opportunity/career policy
Job, freelance, project and contract opportunities are separate types. Zalagren must not silently characterize an opportunity as employment, independent contracting or professional licensing. The underlying relationship and terms must be explicit.

### Messaging
Zalagren-native messaging may support participant and business communication. It must provide:
- conversation authorization
- block/report
- consent where required
- retention controls
- abuse/safety handling
- lawful disclosure workflow

It is not an assertion of WhatsApp integration.

## BeatBnB

BeatBnB is separate from BeatMarket.

BeatBnB coordinates:
Community → Phase → Building → Floor → Unit → Host/Provider/Resident → Property → Guest → Reservation → Authorization → Access → Stay → Evidence.

### Accommodation
A listing must distinguish:
- property/unit relationship
- host/provider authority
- accommodation details
- availability
- price
- cancellation
- reputation
- verification
- compliance
- tax
- complaint path

TRA's current licensing information specifically includes serviced apartments/short-term rentals and provides requirements for tourism enterprises. citeturn0search7

Zalagren does not issue the tourism licence.

### Resident-to-BnB
A resident may propose a unit for BeatBnB only when the participant has the necessary authority over that unit and the applicable community/building/provider/lease/regulatory rules permit the use.

A community role does not automatically confer ownership or host authority.

### Access
A reservation does not itself become permanent physical access.

Guest access must be:
- participant-bound
- unit-bound
- reservation-bound
- time-bound
- authorization-bound
- revocable

Check-in activates the permitted access state and check-out closes it.

### Pricing
BeatBnB may calculate a market observation/range from location, unit attributes, amenities, seasonality, comparable observations, demand and reputation.

It must not represent a generated estimate as an official government price.

### Travel
BeatBnB may coordinate:
- destination discovery
- favorites
- upcoming trips
- accommodation
- flight search
- flight comparison
- private-jet request
- airport/ground transfer
- international itinerary

Live availability and price must come from an actual provider or verified data source. Otherwise the result is clearly marked unknown/request-only.

KCAA states that it licenses air services in Kenya under the Civil Aviation Act and the Air Service Licensing Regulations. citeturn0search4

Therefore Zalagren's initial flight/private-jet architecture is an orchestration/booking boundary, not a claim that Zalagren itself operates aircraft or holds an air-service licence.

## Shared consumer controls

Both domains must support:
- transparent pricing
- terms before consequential confirmation
- cancellation/refund state
- complaint path
- provider identity
- verification/compliance state
- payment state
- evidence
- participant data rights
- security and abuse reporting

No paid Zalagren plan changes a provider's legal duties or a participant's statutory rights.

## Product rule

BeatMarket discovers and connects people, work, businesses, opportunities and commerce.

BeatBnB manages accommodation, stays, access and travel.

A BeatMarket professional/business profile may be used as provider identity in BeatBnB when authorized, but the two products retain separate lifecycle, authorization and compliance state.
