## Kenya launch controls

### Data protection
Zalagren must support purpose limitation, minimisation, rights requests, retention/deletion, access control, audit evidence, controller/processor records, DPIA assessment and cross-border transfer assessment. Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and 2021 regulations remain the primary data-protection framework. ODPC publishes guidance for biometrics, children, health data, cross-border transfers, DPOs, consent and registration.

### Identity
Government document capture is evidence submission, not verification. Verification requires a real verifier/provider, method, timestamp, evidence and scope. Zalagren Identity is platform-issued and is not a government identity.

### Payments
Zalagren coordinates payment intent, authorization, provider execution, reconciliation and evidence. It does not represent itself as a PSP, issuer, deposit-taker, remittance provider or lender unless a separate licensed/regulatory path exists. The September 2026 National Payment System Bill/Policy are tracked as drafts, not current law.

### Tax / commerce
Business participation must expose tax/compliance state without fabricating it. KRA states persons engaged in business are required to onboard eTIMS and issue electronic tax invoices, subject to the applicable rules and exceptions. System-to-system eTIMS integration requires the applicable KRA process/certification.

### Marketplace
Marketplace is a structured commerce coordination domain, not merely a list of cards.

Canonical listing layers:
1. Listing identity — owner, community, jurisdiction.
2. Commercial meaning — goods/service/asset/project/opportunity/capability/accommodation.
3. Offer — price, currency, availability, fulfillment.
4. Trust — verification and compliance state plus evidence.
5. Tax — seller-managed/eTIMS state.
6. Customer protection — terms, complaints, cancellation/refund semantics.
7. Fulfillment — pickup/delivery/appointment/stay/provider dispatch.
8. Transaction — intent/order/reservation/payment boundary.
9. Event/evidence — immutable lifecycle evidence.

No state called "verified" is created without evidence.

### BnB / accommodation
Accommodation is a Marketplace listing kind with a specialized accommodation profile. It must support:
- accommodation type;
- stay type;
- guest capacity;
- rooms/bathrooms where applicable;
- amenities;
- house rules;
- check-in/out;
- approximate/hidden/exact location visibility;
- reservation lifecycle;
- provider reference;
- cancellation/terms;
- evidence.

Exact location is not exposed by default merely because an accommodation exists.

### BeatFood
Merchant identity, food/public-health compliance, menu, order, fulfillment and payment/reconciliation are distinct states. Creating a merchant account does not equal regulatory approval.

### BeatRide
Rider, driver, provider, vehicle/permit/insurance evidence, provider dispatch and trip lifecycle are separate. Zalagren does not grant transport licences.

### Health / Greenhealth
Health data and clinical workflows have a dedicated boundary. Generic marketplace/community authorization cannot grant clinical authority.

### Community / TSAVO
Community representative authority is evidence-based. A participant cannot self-activate representative authority. Representative approval creates explicit participation/context/authorization state.

## Service maturity rule

Every future service must declare:
- purpose;
- participants;
- places/resources;
- capabilities;
- authorization rules;
- regulated-provider boundary;
- data categories;
- lifecycle;
- events/evidence;
- external integrations;
- compliance states;
- failure states;
- user-visible truthful status.

A new service must not bypass the shared ecosystem core.

## Presentation rule

The interface should present the ecosystem by context and capability rather than as disconnected legacy apps. Marketplace must expose mature information hierarchy and truthful state. Prompt-only operations are temporary scaffolding; mature user flows should become structured forms and review surfaces.

## Launch gate

Kenya launch is not declared fully compliant by code alone. Required external gates include applicable ODPC registration/status, provider contracts, tax setup, regulated-provider agreements/licences where applicable, legal terms/privacy notices, security/incident procedures, and any sector-specific approvals required for the actual service scope.

## Status vocabulary

🟢 VERIFIED — evidence checked and current.
🟡 SUPPORTED — architecture/control exists but external evidence or provider activation remains.
🔵 PROPOSED — design exists but implementation is not proven.
🔴 FAILED — control or evidence is missing or invalid.

The UI must never convert 🟡 or 🔵 into 🟢 by wording.
