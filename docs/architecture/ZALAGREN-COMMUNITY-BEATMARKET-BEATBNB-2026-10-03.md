# ZALAGREN COMMUNITY + BEATMARKET + BEATBNB MASTER RECONCILIATION

## Community spatial hierarchy

Canonical hierarchy:
Community → Phase → Building → Floor → Common Area / Unit / Reserved Space → Place

The canonical physical entity remains the existing Place model. parent_id expresses containment and type expresses the semantic level.

Phase: a stage within a community with its own buildings, infrastructure, services and operational context.
Building: a physical structure within a phase or directly within a community.
Floor: a vertical level within a building.
Common Area: lobby, corridor, courtyard, gym, pool, parking, garden, playground, clubhouse or shared utility area.
Unit: private or controlled occupancy space such as apartment, house, office, shop or room.
Reserved Space: a controlled space/resource such as parking bay, meeting room, loading bay or shared facility that requires reservation/authorization.
Place: the generic physical anchor consumed by access, context, services, resources, maintenance, commerce and accommodation.

Community membership does not grant access to every place. A reserved-space reservation is not permanent authority. BeatBnB can reference a unit but cannot change the unit's underlying community authority.

## BeatMarket — separate product

BeatMarket is the participant's professional, opportunity, relationship, business and geographic network.

It combines capabilities inspired by professional networking, freelancing, business operations, social publishing, commerce and geographic discovery, but is a native Zalagren product rather than a clone of LinkedIn, Upwork, Fiverr, Shopify, Alibaba, Instagram, X, Odoo or WhatsApp.

Core lifecycle:
Participant → Professional/Business Profile → Capability/Offer → Opportunity → Relationship → Intent → Authorization → Action → Event → Evidence

BeatMarket supports:
- professional profiles and legal/professional evidence
- skills, services, experience, education and credentials
- careers, jobs, projects, gigs and contracts
- business profiles and public business exposure
- products and services
- professional relationships and messaging
- community and geographic discovery
- static, approximate and explicitly enabled live location
- real place mapping and future 2D/3D spatial presentation
- evidence-backed reputation without a global social score
- commerce and service fulfilment handoff

WhatsApp is an optional external communication channel. Zalagren-native messaging remains the authoritative communication layer. Zalagren must never claim to own or control a participant's WhatsApp account.

Kenya boundary: GPS/location and other sensitive data require privacy controls; the ODPC registration portal explicitly treats GPS location, property/financial information and other sensitive categories as processing considerations. Business registration and beneficial ownership evidence must come from actual BRS records; Zalagren must not invent government registration. Consumer-facing offers must disclose material price/terms and provide complaint handling. ## BeatBnB — separate product

BeatBnB is the accommodation and community-unit coordination network. It is not BeatMarket.

Core relationship:
Community → Phase → Building → Floor → Unit → BeatBnB Property → Provider/Host → Guest/Resident → Stay → Zalagren Access Authorization → Events/Evidence

Roles:
- Unit provider: owner, manager, authorized operator or organization with evidence of the right to offer the unit.
- Host: participant responsible for the stay/guest relationship.
- Guest: participant receiving temporary accommodation and access.
- Resident: participant already connected to the unit/community who may use or transition the unit into an accommodation model where applicable rules permit.
- Community: coordinates local context, common areas, reserved spaces and local rules; it does not automatically become property owner or host.

BeatBnB unit understanding includes phase, building, floor, unit, bedrooms, bathrooms, area, furnishing, parking, utilities, accessibility, safety, inventory, amenities, rules, capacity, check-in/out and inspection evidence.

Pricing intelligence is evidence-based rather than an invented standard price. It compares identified observations using location, unit type, size, rooms, amenities, furnishing, season, demand, duration, community/building context, verified comparable observations, provider history and applicable taxes/fees. The participant should see the observed range and comparable basis before a final provider price.

Access lifecycle:
Guest → Unit → Requested time window → Route/area policy → Authorization → Zalagren access → Entry event → Stay events → Exit event → Authorization expiry/revocation

Reservation does not automatically give unrestricted access to the community. A guest may be authorized for the unit and specifically permitted common areas/reserved spaces while remaining unauthorized elsewhere.

Kenya boundary: hospitality/property-management processing requires appropriate data-protection compliance assessment; ODPC identifies hospitality and property-management among relevant sectors. Consumer pricing, material terms, complaints and refunds must be handled transparently.
