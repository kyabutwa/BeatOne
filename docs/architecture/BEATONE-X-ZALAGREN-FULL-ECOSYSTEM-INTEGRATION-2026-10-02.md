## 1. Brand boundary

Zalagren is the sole current participant-facing ecosystem brand.

BeatOne is retained only as the repository/history lineage and compatibility identifier; it is not a user-facing product identity.

- UI shell, browser title, navigation and participant-facing runtime labels use Zalagren.
- Existing database, migration and internal identifiers containing BeatOne remain unchanged where they are physical compatibility identifiers; they are not user-facing branding.
- GENESIS remains the intelligence layer and never becomes authority.

## 2. Zalagren 8 pillars

1. Identity — participant identity, authenticators, verification boundaries and account security.
2. Access — contextual permissions for places, units, gates and secure digital surfaces.
3. Buildings & Units — canonical spatial hierarchy and place relationships.
4. Payments & Economy — payment intents, regulated provider rails, settlement and reconciliation evidence.
5. Services — utilities, maintenance, concierge and future governed service capabilities.
6. Mobility — BeatRide and provider-bounded mobility flows.
7. Commerce — BeatMarket/Marketplace, BeatFood, accommodation and community commerce.
8. Education & Environment — knowledge, sustainability and future environmental/edge inputs.

## 3. Beat sub-layer naming

Established services: BeatPay, BeatRide, BeatFood, BeatHealth, BeatMarket, BeatGenzi, BeatGuardian, BeatUtilities, and GENESIS.

All services share the canonical identity → participant → context → capability → authorization → intent → proposal → action → event → evidence chain.

The request hub performs local surface routing only. It does not silently authorize or execute actions and does not fabricate GENESIS reasoning or provider execution.

## Community ↔ BeatOne service boundary

BeatOne remains the owner/operator of the ecosystem service layer. Communities do not own, block, disable or globally suspend BeatPay, BeatRide, BeatFood, BeatHealth, BeatMarket, BeatGenzi, BeatGuardian, BeatUtilities or future BeatOne services.

The community management dashboard is a node-level **integration and coordination surface**. Authorized community representatives may configure local integration settings, places, participation context and contextual capabilities. Those settings describe how BeatOne is coordinated inside the node; they do not transfer service ownership or global control to the community.

**Invariant: BeatOne manages the service. The community coordinates the context.**

Real provider execution remains subject to actual provider connections, authorization, compliance and regulated boundaries.
